# Persistent playback pill — manual QA

1. **Navigate while buffering** — Start Play Now on a title, immediately open another season or go Home. Bottom glass pill stays visible with poster, title, quality, and white progress fill.
2. **Player opens when ready** — When buffer completes, full player opens automatically (no need to stay on detail page).
3. **Replace confirmation** — Start Play on title A, then Play on title B (or another torrent). Alert offers to cancel ongoing and start new; Keep Current leaves A running; Start New cancels A and starts B.
4. **Trailer morph** — While torrent buffers, play RT trailer or clip. Player shows trailer; compact progress pill appears top-right; torrent continues in background.
5. **Dismiss trailer** — Close trailer player only; bottom pill reappears if buffer still active. Torrent stream is not cancelled.
6. **Dismiss torrent player** — After torrent playback started, closing player cancels the stream session.
7. **Pill cancel** — Tap X on pill; buffering stops and pill disappears.
